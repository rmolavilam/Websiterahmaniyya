Who We Are

A Tradition of Knowledge, Faith and Character

Rahmaniyya Higher Secondary Madrasa, situated in Olavilam, Kerala, is dedicated to nurturing students through Islamic education, moral development and a meaningful learning experience.

Established on 9 November 1959, our institution continues its educational journey with a commitment to knowledge, discipline, good character and service to the community.

We believe education is not merely the acquisition of information, but also the development of responsible, compassionate and principled individuals.

A Message of Guidance

Education That Shapes Both Knowledge and Character

At Rahmaniyya, we believe that the true purpose of education is to nurture minds, strengthen faith and build character. Every student carries the potential to contribute positively to family, society and the wider world.

Our commitment is to guide students with sincerity, patience and responsibility, helping them grow into knowledgeable, respectful and compassionate individuals.

May Allah bless our students, teachers, parents and all those who contribute to the continued success of our institution.

— Shameer Nizami
Sadar Usthad, Rahmaniyya Higher Secondary Madrasa
